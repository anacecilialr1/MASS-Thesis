## Master Thesis
This Master thesis is submitted in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the degree Master in Astrophysics and Space Science, as part of a multiple degree awarded in the framework of the Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in Astrophysics and Space Science −− MASS, jointly delivered by a Consortium of four Universities: Tor Vergata University of Rome, University of Belgrade, University of Bremen, and Université Cote d’Azur, regulated by the MASS Consortium Agreement and funded by the EU under the call ERASMUS-EDU-2021-PEX-EMJM-MOB.

### Master Thesis Administration — University of Belgrade, Faculty of Mathematics

Official process (in Serbian): https://teze.matf.bg.ac.rs/master/

### Key Deadlines
|---|---|
| Topic application submitted | May 15, 2026 |
| Final manuscript submitted | August 31, 2026 |
| Thesis defense | September 30, 2026 |

### Process

1. **Prepare topic application**
 — Fill out `molba_master_formular_eng.doc` (English version of the official form) in agreement with mentor.
 - *External committee members* (not employed at MATF): supervisor sends completed form 

2. **Submit application officially** (by May 15) — via [StudInfo](https://hypatia.matf.bg.ac.rs:10333/StudInfo/scripts/studenti/prijavljivanjeFormular). Interface is in Serbian; text entered can be in English. [Screenshot guide (PDF)](https://tezefiles.matf.bg.ac.rs/master_files/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/podnosenjeMolbePrijavaTemeMasterRada.pdf).

3. **Approval** — Application reviewed by the Department of Astronomy and the Teaching & Research Council. Notification by email once accepted.

4. **Write & submit thesis** (by August 31)
   - Final submission is done online via StudInfo.
   - Only minor edits allowed after this point.

5. **Review, report & defense**
   - Committee prepares assessment report; mentor uploads it via StudInfo to the teze portal.
   - Report must be approved by the Department of Astronomy.
   - Defense possible once report is approved **and** all program exams are passed/recorded.
   - **Printed copies required before defense**: one per committee member + one for the Faculty library (A4, 210×297mm), library copy delivered to **office 707**.
